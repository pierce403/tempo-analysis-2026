Claim unit and evidence contract
--------------------------------
A report claim unit is one metadata line, prose paragraph, list item, or table data row. Headings,
blank lines, table headers/separators, and fenced-code-only lines are exempt. Every claim unit must
contain its own inline Markdown link below evidence/. Every linked narrative .txt must contain an
observation/execution date or pin, a safe re-run command/procedure, a captured result, and explicit
limitations/safety. Scripts, tests, and patches referenced for reproduction must be Git-tracked.

Review limitations
------------------
The validator proves structural traceability, file existence/tracking, and the presence of the four
required evidence fields; it cannot prove that a command was honestly executed or that analysis is
correct. Those properties require review of captured output, pinned source, limitations, and safe
independent reproduction. Recommendations, severity, threat scenarios, and prioritization remain
analytical judgments. Public snapshots can drift after their recorded timestamps, and private
controls remain owner-confirmation items.
